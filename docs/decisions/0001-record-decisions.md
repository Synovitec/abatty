# 0001 - Record the decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-19

## Context

A decision nobody wrote down is taken again by the next person, or by the next session, usually
differently. The cost is not the first decision; it is the third time it is re-argued.

## Decision

Every decision that constrains the code lands here as its own file, numbered in order, and is
never deleted. A decision that is reversed gets a new file that supersedes the old one, and the
old one stays.

## Consequences

The record grows and is never tidied. That is the point: what was true and why it changed is the
part a reader needs, and a tidy record has lost it.
