# Evidence against the essential requirements

## Read this first

> A mapping is not a conformity assessment and is not legal advice. The wording here is a short paraphrase for orientation; conformity is judged against the regulation's own text, about a product in a market, by people with the whole picture.

The source: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (the Cyber Resilience Act). Annex I: Part I, the product's properties; Part II, vulnerability handling. The requirement titles below are a short paraphrase for orientation and are **not** the regulation's wording; the references are there so you go and read it.

Of 21 requirements, **7 have no rule in this catalog that bears on them at all**, and 14 have at least one. That first number is the useful one, and it is why the empty requirements are listed before the others on this page.
